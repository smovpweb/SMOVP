Privacy Notice

Last updated: 21-Nov-2026

1. Who we are

This website is run by St. Mary's Orthodox Valiyapally, Jalahalli, a parish of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church, Bangalore Diocese ("the parish", "we", "us"). Our address is Gangamma Circle, Church Road, Ramachandrapura, Jalahalli East, Bengaluru – 560013, Karnataka. You can reach us at office.smovp@gmail.com or 080-28386109.

2. The short version

We do not ask you for any personal information on this website. There are no forms, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The only thing the site itself remembers is your language choice (English or Malayalam), and that stays on your own device.

4. Services provided by others

Some parts of the page are delivered by other companies. When your browser loads them, those companies receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page you are on, under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Google privacy policy.
  • Google Maps shows the map in the Visit section. The embedded map may set Google cookies. Google privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare serves the website and may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security. Cloudflare privacy policy.

5. Links to other websites

The site links to other services. We do not control them, and their own privacy policies apply once you follow a link:

  • Member login and the Parish Information Centre are provided by ChurchGovern (churchgovern.app and smovp.churchgovern.com). Any details you enter there are handled by ChurchGovern and the parish under [ChurchGovern's privacy policy / the parish's member data policy].
  • Our Facebook, YouTube and Instagram pages.
  • The Bangalore Diocese and Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church websites.

6. If you contact us

If you phone us or write to office.smovp@gmail.com, we use what you send only to reply and to look after parish matters you raise. We keep it only as long as needed for that purpose, unless church or legal records require longer.

7. Children

The website does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 18.

8. Contact and grievances

For any privacy question or complaint, contact the Parish Secretary at office.smovp@gmail.com or 080-28386109. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Changes

We may update this notice when the website changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
